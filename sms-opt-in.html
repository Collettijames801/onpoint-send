SMS Opt-In Process

On Point Home Inspection Services LLC

How clients opt in

Clients book home inspections with On Point Home Inspection Services LLC by phone. During the booking call, the inspector asks for the client's mobile number and reads the following consent script. Texts are only sent to clients who say yes.

Verbal consent script

"Can I text you a short form to fill out before your inspection? You'll get a text from On Point Home Inspection with the form link, and maybe a reminder or appointment update. Message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime to opt out or HELP for help. Is that okay?"

Record of consent

When the client agrees, the inspector notes the date of consent with the client's booking before sending the first text.

First message the client receives

"Hi Sarah, thanks for booking your home inspection with On Point Home Inspection Services! Please fill out this quick form before your appointment: [form link] Reply STOP to opt out."
