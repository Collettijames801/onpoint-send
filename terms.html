On Point Home Inspection Services LLC · Text Messaging Program · Last updated November 6, 2026

Program description

On Point Home Inspection Services LLC sends text messages to clients about their scheduled home inspection, including intake forms to complete before the inspection and appointment information.

How you opt in

You agree to receive these text messages by providing your mobile number when you book an inspection with us by phone. During the call, we let you know you'll receive a text with a form to fill out.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, typically a few messages per inspection. Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll receive one confirmation message, and no further messages after that. To start again, reply START.

Help

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties for marketing purposes.
